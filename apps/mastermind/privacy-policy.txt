Mastermind Privacy Policy
=========================

Effective date: 2026-11-17
Application: Mastermind (Senha) for Android (asfernandes.senha2)
Developer: Adriano dos Santos Fernandes
Contact: adrianosf@gmail.com


1. Overview
-----------

Mastermind is a game that can be fully played offline. It has no accounts and
no ads. The app can optionally send anonymous usage statistics to Google
Analytics, as described in section 3. We operate no servers of our own.


2. Data stored on your device
-----------------------------

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

- Game data: win and loss counts and the best records for each game mode (date
  and time of the match, time taken and number of tries).
- Your choice about usage statistics and, if statistics are enabled, a random
  identifier generated by the app and a short queue of events waiting to be
  sent.

This data never leaves your device, except for the usage statistics described
below. It is not included in Android backups.


3. Usage statistics (Google Analytics)
--------------------------------------

If usage statistics are enabled, the app sends the following to Google
Analytics (a service of Google LLC), to help us understand how the game is
used and improve it:

- screens viewed;
- when a game starts and ends, the game mode (for example 4x6), the result
  (won, gave up or abandoned), the number of tries and the time taken;
- the app version;
- a random identifier generated by the app and a session number.

The app does not send your name, e-mail, contacts, location, advertising
identifier or any other identifier of your device or account. Because the data
is sent over the internet, Google receives your IP address as part of the
connection. Google uses it to derive approximate location (such as country) and
does not store it in the reports we see.

Google processes this data on our behalf and may do so in countries other than
yours, under safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard
contractual clauses. See https://policies.google.com/privacy for details.

Legal basis:
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, statistics
  are sent only if you consent. The app asks on first use and nothing is sent
  before you accept (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
- In other countries, statistics are enabled by default, based on our
  legitimate interest in improving the game (LGPD Art. 7, IX), and you can turn
  them off at any time.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Usage statistics" switch in
the settings (gear button at the bottom right of the main screen). Turning
statistics off stops all sending and deletes the random identifier and any
events not yet sent. If the country of your device cannot be determined, the
app behaves as in the European Economic Area and asks first.


4. Sharing
----------

We do not sell data. Google Analytics, described above, is the only service
that receives data, and only when usage statistics are enabled.


5. Retention and deletion
-------------------------

Game data and the random identifier are kept on your device until you clear
the app's data or uninstall the app. Usage statistics are kept in Google
Analytics for 14 months and then deleted automatically.


6. Your rights
--------------

Under the LGPD and the GDPR you may ask us to confirm, access, correct or
delete your data, and may withdraw consent or object to processing at any time.
The statistics are linked only to a random identifier, so we can act on a
request only if you can tell us this identifier; deleting the app's data or
turning statistics off already breaks any link to you. Contact us at the
address above for any request.


7. Children
-----------

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal
data from children. Parents who prefer that no statistics are sent can turn them
off in the app.


8. Changes
----------

We will update this policy when the app or the law changes, and update the
effective date above.
