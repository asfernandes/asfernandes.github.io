Mastermind Privacy Policy
=========================

Effective date: 2026-11-16
Application: Mastermind (Senha) for Android (asfernandes.senha2)
Developer: Adriano dos Santos Fernandes
Contact: adrianosf@gmail.com


1. Overview
-----------

Mastermind is an offline game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics
and no network access. We operate no servers and never receive any data
from you or your device.


2. Data stored on your device
-----------------------------

The app keeps only game data in its private storage on your device:
win and loss counts and the best records for each game mode (date and
time of the match, time taken and number of tries).

This data is not personal and never leaves your device. It is not
included in Android backups.


3. Sharing
----------

We do not collect, sell or share any data.


4. Retention and deletion
-------------------------

Game data is kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.


5. Children
-----------

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.


6. Changes
----------

We will update this policy when the app or the law changes, and update
the effective date above.
