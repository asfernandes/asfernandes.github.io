Pirueto Privacy Policy
======================

Effective date: 2026-11-12
Application: Pirueto for Android (io.github.asfernandes.pirueto)
Developer: Adriano dos Santos Fernandes
Contact: adrianosf@gmail.com


1. Overview
-----------

Pirueto is an offline game. We operate no servers and no accounts, so
we never receive your game data. Your data stays on your device, except
for what the Google services described in Section 3 process.


2. Data stored on your device
-----------------------------

The app keeps its data in its private storage on your device: settings,
the players you create (the names you type, their options), progress,
best-run replays and the list of purchased level packs.

Player names are free text; you do not need to use your real name.


3. Google services
------------------

3.1 Google Analytics (optional usage data)

The app may send anonymous usage data to Google Analytics, such as
which screens and levels are played, game events, error reports,
settings changes and basic device information (device type, language,
screen size, operating system, app version), with a random identifier
generated by the app. It never sends player names, text you type or
payment data, and does not use the advertising ID. Data is marked as
not usable for advertising. Google receives your IP address when data
is sent.

Usage data is on by default, except where the law requires consent, in
which case the game asks first. You can turn it off at any time in the
game settings; doing so also deletes the random identifier from your
device. Data already sent is kept by Google Analytics for a limited
period and then deleted automatically.

3.2 Google Play Billing

Optional level packs are sold through Google Play. Google processes the
purchase and payment; the app never sees your payment details and only
learns which packs you own.

3.3 Google Play Games Services

When you are signed in to Play Games, each player's name, progress and
replays are saved to your Google account so they sync across your
devices. Deleting a player in the game also deletes its cloud save.

3.4 Android backup

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up the
app's data to your Google account. This is controlled by your device
settings.

These services are provided by Google under its own policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy


4. Sharing
----------

We do not sell or share your data. Data reaches third parties only as
described in Section 3.


5. Retention and deletion
-------------------------

On-device data is kept until you delete it in the game or uninstall the
app. Cloud saves, backups and purchase records can also be managed in
your Google account. Because we hold no data about you, there is no
account to delete.


6. Children
-----------

Pirueto is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect
personal data from children.


7. Your rights
--------------

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct,
delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to
withdraw consent. Most of these are exercised directly in the game or
in your Google account. Contact us for help, or complain to your local
data protection authority.

For users in the EU and UK, we rely on your consent for usage data where
the game asks for it and on our legitimate interest in improving the
game elsewhere, and on the performance of a contract for purchases and
cloud save.


8. Changes
----------

We will update this policy when the app or the law changes, and update
the effective date above.
